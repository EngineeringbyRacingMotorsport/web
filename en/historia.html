Our History

Since 2017, we have built five single-seater race cars, from gasoline engines to batteries and an electric motor. We have been a small team that has never been afraid of change, always wanting to learn, improve, and strive to build with pride.

EM-06

The EM-06 was the team’s first electric car to compete in the Autocross and Endurance dynamic events at the Formula Student Spain competition. Furthermore, it demonstrated strong international performance by securing 2nd place among the participating Spanish teams at Formula Student France.

EM-05

The team's second electric single-seater. It competed in two events: Formula Student Spain and Formula Student Portugal. For the first time, it managed to pass two of the three inspections, coming very close to being able to race. This car allowed us to conduct testing, research, and development to prepare for the new single-seater.

EM-04

Faced with the change of direction Formula Student Germany had taken to eliminate the combustion category, the team decided to electrify our single-seater. This decision posed a great challenge, and thus the EM-04 was born, with which we went to Formula Student Spain where we failed to pass scrutineering.

EM-03

With the improvements to the single-seater and the experience gained, the EM-03 already stood out in competition, achieving first place in the efficiency trial at the Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya and second place at Formula Student Austria, where it was competing for the first time.

EM-02

Following the success of the first car, the second single-seater was the first to attend two competitions, one of them Formula Student Spain and the other in Italy at the Ricardo Paletti circuit, the first time the team attended an international competition.

EM-01

With the team's founding and two years of development, this was the team's first single-seater with an internal combustion engine. It participated in Formula Student Spain at the Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya, successfully passing scrutineering in its first year of competition.
