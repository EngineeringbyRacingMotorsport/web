Who are we?

We are the Formula Student team of the Salesian University School of Sarrià, founded in 2017. During these years, we have built five different race cars. In 2022, the team made a change by switching to an electric-powered single-seater. Since then, we have continued to learn and fight for electric mobility. We are a group of students from different engineering degree programs, united by a passion for racing and competition. We work as a team to design, build, and compete with our single-seater.
